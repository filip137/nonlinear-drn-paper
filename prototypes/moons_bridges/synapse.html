Local sensing

Why the bridge senses a square

Write the branch drop as u = VA − VB and its midpoint as m = (VA + VB)/2. Two unit junctions sense the endpoints. A double-area junction senses the midpoint.

Charge of one reverse-biased junction
q(v) = 2Cj0φ [√(1 + (v − VS)/φ) − 1]
Signal minus reference charge
Q = q(m + u/2) + q(m − u/2) − 2q(m)
Q ≈ ¼ q″(m) u²

The linear terms cancel. The curvature q″ is negative, so a larger drop magnitude gives a more negative Q at fixed midpoint.

Two equilibria · one input

What the nudge changes

Hold the input fixed. Let d be the positive output voltage minus the negative output voltage, and y the target class (−1 or +1).

Free phase

The network settles on its own

vf = arg minv E(v, g)

Each edge sees a free drop uf. One shared circuit samples df. It requests a write when y df < 30 µV.

Nudged phase · requested writes

The teacher loads the outputs

vn = arg minv [E(v, g) + ½β(d − yVT)²]

A 120 µS teaching conductance pulls d toward yVT, with VT = 140 mV. The new equilibrium changes the branch drop to un.

Ideal equilibrium-propagation update
Eedge = ½g u²   ⇒   ΔgEP = η2β (uf² − un²)

This is the small-nudge gradient estimate with g fixed during relaxation and learning rate η. In the simulated writer, β is finite and W can move during the nudge.

Charge transfer

What remains on the capacitor

At write enable
Wa = CWWold + CbVparkCW + Cb

The 2 fF parked bus shares charge with the 0.33 pF store. Wa is the armed voltage in the reduced model.

During the nudge
Wnew ≈ Wa + Qn − QfCW + Cb

At similar midpoint voltages, Qn − Qf is proportional to uf² − un². A smaller nudged drop magnitude therefore raises W and strengthens the read conductance.

From one write to a classifier

1,016 correct in a fixed-weight ngspice DC test. In the uninterrupted transient, 127 of the first 128 test points were correct with writes disabled.

36 bridges, 12 diode cells

The circuit trained on 64 examples over eight epochs. The shared margin gate enabled 51 of 512 write windows. Enabling every window gave 93.07% on the same test.
